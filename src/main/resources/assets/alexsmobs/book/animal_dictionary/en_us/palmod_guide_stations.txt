Craft a Pal Work Station and place it anywhere. It is a plain 27-slot chest: hoppers, comparators and your own hands all work on it.
<NEWLINE>
Some Pals have a job. Throw one out near its work and it starts on its own - no assignment needed.
<NEWLINE>
Jobs include Harvester, Lumberjack, Miner, Trader and Sorter.
<NEWLINE>
A working Pal carries what it gathers and hauls a full load to the NEAREST Work Station it can find. With none in range it simply holds onto the load.
<NEWLINE>
A worker Pal takes its sphere WITH it, so it keeps working while you are away. Right-click the Pal to call it back into its sphere. If it dies it drops an empty sphere where it fell, and breaking a station never harms it.
